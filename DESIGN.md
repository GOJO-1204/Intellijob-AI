# IntelliJob design rules

Read this before you build any screen, email or page. It applies to people and to AI tools.

## Product
- Name: IntelliJob (earlier called JobMatch AI).
- Contact email: mangarajbibek1209@gmail.com

## Colors and shape
- Accent color: teal #0F766E.
- Hover color: #115E59.
- Status colors: red, amber, green.
- Fonts: system font stack. No web fonts.
- Corner radius: 6 to 8px.
- Motion: minimal.

## Never do
- No purple.
- No gradients.
- No pill shaped buttons.
- No fake metrics, fake reviews or fake testimonials. Numbers on screen must be real counts from the database.
- No emoji used as icons.
- No dashes used as punctuation in user facing copy. That means no em dashes, no en dashes and no spaced hyphens as a pause. Use a full stop or a comma instead.
- No AI slop copy and no stock "AI" images.
- No "AI generated" tag or badge anywhere.
- No over the top animation.
- The site must not look AI generated.
- A dark purple gradient design spec called "Synapse" was rejected because it breaks these rules. Never bring it back.

## Copy style
- Use short, plain, factual sentences.
- Say what happened and what the user can do next.
- Good copy that already exists in the app: heading "Alerts paused", text "You will no longer get job alert emails."

## Where these rules apply
- Every screen.
- Every email.
- The legal pages (Privacy, Terms).
- The favicon and brand assets.

## Check before you open a PR
Answer yes to every question. If any answer is no, fix it first.

1. Is the accent teal #0F766E, with #115E59 on hover?
2. Are the status colors red, amber and green?
3. Is the screen free of purple and gradients?
4. Are corners 6 to 8px, with no pill shaped buttons?
5. Does it use only system fonts and minimal motion?
6. Is every number a real count from the database, with no fake reviews or testimonials?
7. Is it free of emoji icons, stock "AI" images, AI slop copy and any "AI generated" badge?
8. Is the copy short, plain and factual, with no dashes used as punctuation, and does it say what the user can do next?
